# Streakly — Play Store listing (draft)

## App name
Streakly — Habit Tracker

## Short description (80 chars)
Build habits one day at a time. Streaks, calendar heatmap and reminders — offline.

## Full description
Streakly is a simple, beautiful habit tracker that lives entirely on your
phone.

**Build a streak, one day at a time**
Create a habit in seconds — pick an icon, a colour and a streak target —
then check in every day. Watch your flame grow as the streak builds.

**See your progress**
- Current and best streaks for every habit
- A GitHub-style heatmap of the last 16 weeks
- A full month calendar you can tap to check in past days
- Weekly and monthly stats, per habit and overall

**Never forget**
Set a daily reminder time per habit and Streakly nudges you at the same
time every day.

**Your notes, your journey**
Attach a note to any day — what helped, what got in the way — and read
them back as a timeline on each habit.

**Private by design**
Everything is stored on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, no
internet needed. The app starts empty and stays yours.

Streakly is for anyone building a small daily habit: reading, workouts,
meditation, drinking water, studying, saving money — whatever matters to
you, one day at a time.

## Privacy policy note
Streakly collects no data. All habits, check-ins and notes are stored
locally on the device in a SQLite database and never leave the phone.
